# English Creative Commons prenatal videos: shortlist and first picks

Every video here showed **"Creative Commons Attribution licence (reuse allowed)"** on its YouTube page (re-checked 2026-10-29). Spoken language was checked from YouTube's auto-generated caption language. Nothing is downloaded. Each pick has a licence screenshot in [legal/evidence/](../legal/evidence/).

> **How the picks were made.** I made these choices as an editorial draft, weighing the labour-room setting (nurse-operated, short, equipment-free), WHO's positive-childbirth guidance (relaxation, companion support, free movement and position) and Cochrane evidence on continuous support. I chose from each video's title, description, channel and length; **I have not watched them**, and I am not a clinician. Your clinical advisory board must review every video before release.
