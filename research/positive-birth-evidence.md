## 2. Evidence by practice (maps to the four modules)

### Birth companion / continuous support (Learn)
- Cochrane review CD003766: continuous one-to-one support gave more spontaneous vaginal births (RR 1.08, 95% CI 1.04 to 1.12; 21 trials, 14,369 women) and less intrapartum analgesia (RR 0.90). No harms found. Evidence certainty is rated low. Largest benefit with a doula and where epidurals are not routine.
  [Cochrane summary](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003766/continuous-support-women-during-childbirth) · [Full review](https://cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD003766.pub6)

### Eating and drinking in labour (Hydration Check, Learn)
- WHO 2018: oral fluid and food recommended for low-risk women. The recommendation is conditional and the evidence certainty is very low. IV fluids to shorten labour are not recommended.
  [BIGG recommendation record](https://bigg-rec.bvsalud.org/en/recommendations/1475cb0e7757988dc6c63a860723d9299049939f)
- Cochrane CD003930 (5 trials, 3,130 women): no benefit or harm from restricting food and fluids in women at low risk of needing anaesthesia. Women should be free to eat and drink as they wish.
  [Cochrane summary](https://www.cochrane.org/CD003930/PREG_eating-and-drinking-in-labour) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20091553/)
- **Fernandez Hospital, Hyderabad:** prospective observational study of 211 low-risk women compared clear calorific fluids with solids plus fluids. No significant difference in obstetric or neonatal outcomes. Authors conclude eating in labour is safe for low-risk pregnancies.
  [Indian Journal of Nutrition and Dietetics](https://informaticsjournals.com/index.php/ijnd/article/view/25762)
- Practice gap: a 2024 Ghana study found providers often restrict intake despite the WHO recommendation, so a reminder tool has a real behaviour-change target.
  [BMC Preg & Childbirth 2024](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11138096/)
- ⚠ **No published threshold was found for a temperature/pulse hydration prompt or a fixed fluid-offer interval.** The PRD already says thresholds must be clinically validated. Until the advisory board sets them, the app should ship the interval as a configurable facility protocol setting, not a built-in clinical rule.

### Music (Sound & Calm)
- 2020 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (1,310 women): music may reduce anxiety scores and anxiety-related physiological indices.
  [PubMed 33050409](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33050409/)
- 2025 meta-analysis of 9 RCTs: pain reductions in latent, active and second stages; no significant anxiety effect in latent/active phases.
  [PMC12065334](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12065334/)
- ⚠ No Cochrane review on music in labour was found. Evidence is low quality. Position music as comfort and relaxation, never treatment (matches the PRD).
- ⚠ WHO 2018 lists music among relaxation techniques for women who request pain relief. Confirm the exact recommendation number in the WHO text.

### Antenatal movement and pelvic exercises (Move & Ease)
- Meta-analysis (Sobhgol et al., 2020): antenatal pelvic floor exercise may shorten second stage and reduce severe perineal trauma, but most trials had moderate to high risk of bias. Individual trials are mixed (some show no effect; adherence was often low).
  [Western Sydney trial](https://researchers.westernsydney.edu.au/en/publications/the-effect-of-antenatal-pelvic-floor-muscle-exercises-on-labour-a/) · [2025 RCT, PMC12346635](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12346635/)
- ⚠ Not yet searched: birth-ball / upright-position evidence, and WHO's mobility and position recommendation text.
- Clinical note: exercises need gestational-age and contraindication screening by the advisory board (PRD requirement).

### Respectful care and the Fernandez Hospital model
- Fernandez Hospital opened India's first dedicated birthing suite (Dr. Lourdes C. Fernandez Birthing Suite, Bogulkunta) on 9 June 2019.
  [Hospital blog](https://www.fernandezhospital.com/blogs/dr-lourdes-c-fernandez-birthing-suite)
- Fernandez Foundation and UNFPA hosted the *Better Birthing Experience* conference (Nov 2023).
- Dr. Evita Fernandez TEDx talk: [Natural Birthing, a Promise of Dignity in Birth](https://www.ted.com/talks/dr_evita_fernandez_natural_birthing_a_promise_of_dignity_in_birth)
- Training study (UBC-linked): [88 midwives, doulas, physiotherapists and obstetricians trained in acupressure for labour pain](https://maa.med.ubc.ca/?p=1438)
- ⚠ No single named "positive birth experience programme" page was found. Request the hospital's protocol documents (companion policy, music policy, oral-intake policy) directly.
