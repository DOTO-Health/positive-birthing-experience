## Before you use any of these

1. **The licence is the uploader's own claim.** YouTube's CC BY setting is chosen by whoever uploads. A video can contain third-party music or clips the uploader had no right to relicense. Watch each one and note any background music; reject it if you hear commercial music.
2. **Attribution is required** (CC BY): credit the channel, link the video and name the licence. Suggested line: "<title>" by <channel>, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>, licensed under CC BY (YouTube Creative Commons Attribution licence). The app's `videos.json` already carries `author`, `source_url` and `license` for the eight shortlisted entries.
3. **CC BY fits inside your CC BY-SA content plan** but stays CC BY: keep the credit, and do not imply the creator endorses the app.
4. **Clinical review first.** These are mostly yoga and fitness channels, not clinical sources. The advisory board must check trimester suitability, contraindications, and whether positions (squats, duck walk, birth-ball work) are appropriate.
5. **Downloading:** YouTube's terms generally do not allow downloading except through its own features, even for CC BY videos. The practical route is to ask the creator for the original file in writing (many are individuals or small studios), which also settles the music question. A tool such as [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) can fetch a file and write the uploader and licence into an `.info.json` for your record. It is not installed here and nothing was downloaded.
6. **Language fit:** the best Indian-language matches are Yoga With Sayali (Marathi/English), Health and fitness world (Hindi), Aadhan Tamil (Tamil, presented by a doctor) and Bedekar Hospital (Marathi). Kannada, Telugu and Malayalam searches returned few antenatal exercise videos.
